The Spot

Drive through the hay. Park under the oaks. Exhale.

Coyote Camp is a single, private campsite in a small grove of Oregon white oak, right on the bank of Coyote Creek.

You'll roll through an open hay field to reach it — keep your eyes open on the way in, because this land is full of wildlife. The grove itself gives you deep, cool shade on summer afternoons, a picnic table and benches under the trees, and a big stone fire ring for when the county fire ban lifts.

There are fish in the creek and it's a favourite swimming hole for friendly dogs. Beyond the grove there's plenty of acreage to wander, watch and explore — and at night, it's just you, the crickets and the coyotes.

Best of all, you're not really that far from anything: downtown Eugene is about 15–20 minutes away, Fern Ridge and the local wineries are close by, and the Oregon coast is roughly an hour's drive.
